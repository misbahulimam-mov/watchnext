# WatchNext Content Guide

This file explains where the editorial content lives.

## Main content

The easiest way to update the site is to edit the data arrays near the top of **script.js**:

- `indiaPicks` — Indian movies and shows shown on the homepage.
- `releases` — New releases.
- `lists` — Top 10 / Top 5 curated lists.
- `reviews` — Editorial reviews.
- `titleDetails` — richer title information such as cast, trailer, availability and similar titles.

## Indian-language coverage

WatchNext is intended to be pan-India. Current homepage filters include Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi and Punjabi. Add more languages whenever content is available.

## Review format

Each review can include:

- title
- score
- spoiler-free summary
- headline
- what works
- things to know
- who it is for
- WatchNext verdict

Keep reviews spoiler-free on the homepage and in the title modal.
